Costs and payments

What you pay for, and what you pay as you run it

The development price covers building and launching the five apps. Hosting, SMS, payment fees and a few other services are paid to the providers, not to us. This page shows each one, roughly what it costs in taka, and how the payment methods compare. All amounts are estimates to plan with. Providers confirm the final rates.

Three kinds of cost

One build, then a small monthly bill, then a small cut of each online payment

1

Development

Paid once, in phases, as agreed with us. Quoted separately.

  • Five apps and the admin panel
  • Backend, realtime, maps, chat
  • Testing, store release, handover
2

Running costs

Paid monthly or yearly to the providers once the apps are live.

  • Hosting and database
  • SMS codes, push, storage
  • Domain, store accounts
3

Per transaction

Taken by the payment provider and the courier on each order.

  • bKash, Nagad and card fees
  • Courier charge per parcel
  • Cash handling for cash on delivery

Payment methods

Which methods to offer, and what each one costs you

Customers in Bangladesh pay in cash, with bKash, with Nagad and with cards. Each has trade-offs. Rates below come from provider pages and third-party listings and differ by source, so treat them as a guide until the merchant agreement is signed.

What the fee means in taka

A percentage fee is small on a service and large on an air conditioner. This is why high-value orders need another route.

Recommended

Start with cash plus one gateway, then go direct

  1. Day one: cash on delivery and SSLCommerz. One agreement and one integration give you bKash, Nagad, Rocket and cards. The apps already treat the gateway as a swappable part.
  2. When the bKash agreement is ready: add the direct bKash API behind the same switch. A direct contract usually costs less per payment and gives one-tap tokenised checkout, so repeat customers pay faster.
  3. For air conditioners: offer bank transfer and card instalments or a deposit, because a percentage fee on ৳65,000 is real money.
  4. Offers: bKash and Nagad run cashback campaigns that they fund. Ask for them in the agreement instead of paying for discounts yourself.

Technology is the fast part. These items take days or weeks and sit with you or the providers. Starting them in the first month keeps launch on time.

Sources and caveats

Where the numbers come from

Rates change and differ by contract. Nothing here is an offer from a provider. Before signing, get written quotes for SMS, the payment gateway, courier, hosting and any identity verification.
SMS prices: Alpha SMS pricing (non-masking about ৳0.31 to 0.40, masking about ৳0.56 to 0.64 per SMS, by recharge size).
Payment fees: card about 2.5% and mobile wallets about 1.2% to 2.1% depending on the source, for example this gateway comparison. bKash publishes its checkout terms at bkash.com but not a merchant rate.
Domain: BTCL announced a cut of the .com.bd fee, see The Financial Express. Resellers charge more.
Identity checks against the NID record are optional and paid. Access is reported to be limited to approved entities, see this provider note (a vendor claim). The launch plan checks identity with photos, a live selfie and human review, which costs nothing per check.
Hosting, push and store fees follow the providers' public price lists in US dollars.
