Hosting for smooth apps

We recommend a fast tier from day one, not the cheapest plan. The live map, chat and notifications need a server that answers quickly.

  • Region: Singapore or Mumbai, the closest to Dhaka.
  • Server: dedicated API server, 2 CPU and 4 GB, with room to add more.
  • Database: managed Postgres with location search and daily backups.
  • Fast layer: Redis for live tracking and chat, CDN for images.
  • Before launch: load test with thousands of simulated users (included in development).
  • Later: automatic scaling when bookings grow. Examples: ৳11,000 to 16,000 a month at launch, about ৳36,000 to 48,000 when growing.

4. Payments

Customers pay NEW directly, online. No cash goes to technicians. Small payments use mobile wallets, large payments use cards or bank transfer.

How the customer pays

  • Booking confirmed: minimum visit charge is paid first.
  • Technician inspects, NEW sends an estimate: parts plus service charge.
  • Customer approves and pays online, then work starts.
  • Cash stays available as a fallback. Online payers get a reward, so they choose it.

Which method for which amount

  • Under ৳5,000: bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay.
  • ৳5,000 to ৳30,000: wallets or card.
  • AC purchase, ৳30,000 and above: card through a gateway, or bank transfer (no percentage fee).
