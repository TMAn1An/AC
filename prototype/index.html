The core idea

Nearest technician first, vendor second

Admin keeps control of every job. If a NEW technician is within the service radius, the job goes straight to them. If not, it is forwarded to the closest vendor, who assigns one of their own technicians. Everyone sees each step.

Design sprint

What we learned before drawing a single screen

Desk research on the products our users already know, distilled into five rules that every screen in these prototypes follows.

Service booking

Urban Company

Upfront price, a time slot picker and a clear professional card before anything is confirmed.

Adopted: fixed prices and slots in the booking form.
Live tracking

Uber and Pathao

ETA is the headline. Map and contact shortcuts sit directly under it.

Adopted: ETA-first status card, call and WhatsApp buttons.
Order progress

foodpanda

A short vertical timeline that always shows what is done, what is happening and what is next.

Adopted: one timeline for bookings and orders.
Field operations

ServiceTitan and Jobber

Dispatchers work from a board plus a detail drawer, never from a wall of forms.

Adopted: bookings table with a dispatch drawer and live map.
Commerce

Daraz

Powerful but dense. The client wants a calmer store with only three sections.

Adopted: three sections, large product art, no clutter.
Dashboards

Stripe and Linear

Quiet neutrals, hairline borders and one brand color that is saved for actions.

Adopted: restrained palette, table-first admin.

Brand and logo

From the client logo

Same six blades, same order, same three colours. The refined version has clean vector geometry, even spacing and a crisp wordmark, and it works from 16 px up to print.

Colour ratio 60 / 30 / 10

White and cool grey carry the screens. Logo blue is for navigation, headers and primary actions. Red is for promotions, alerts and unread. Green is for success and live status. The glossy finish comes from light edges, soft coloured shadows and glass blur, never gradients.

Build plan

One Flutter codebase family, one realtime backend

The prototypes are static HTML so they can be reviewed anywhere. The product build follows the decisions below. The full production plan covers scope, rules, Android specifics, quality gates, phases and risks.

Read the production plan

Customer appFlutter, Android first. Shop, service booking, live tracking, orders, support chat.
Technician appFlutter, Android. Job offers, trip with foreground location service, extra work, completion, cash.
Vendor appFlutter, Android. Forwarded jobs, assign own technicians, team, earnings and settlement.
Admin appFlutter, Android phone and tablet. Dispatch, approvals, inbox, live map, collections on the go.
Admin web appFlutter Web. Catalog, orders, finance, settings, user management.
ArchitectureMVVM: views and ChangeNotifier view models, repositories and services, optional use cases. One workspace, shared core packages.
BackendPostgreSQL with PostGIS for nearest-technician queries, row level security per role, realtime channels for status and location.
MapsOpenStreetMap tiles through flutter_map. No Google APIs. Location shared only while a job is on the way.
PaymentsCash on delivery first. SSLCommerz (bKash, Nagad, cards) behind a gateway interface with webhook confirmation.
Delivery and contactThird-party couriers with tracking id. Technician contact by call or WhatsApp. Customer to admin by in-app chat.
