Why the technician needs an app. The business depends on the technician being reached in 60 seconds and customers seeing the technician on the way. A website cannot do either when the phone is locked in the technician's pocket. Everything else works well on the web.

Our recommendation

Option 2: website for customers, vendors and admin, Android app for technicians.
  • It costs about the same as website only, because the technician screens must be built either way.
  • Customers get a shop Google can find, with no app to install.
  • Technicians get the alerts and live location that the service depends on.
  • iPhone apps for customers or vendors can be added later if you need them, at a price agreed then.

How to choose

  • Do customers search on Google before they buy? If yes, the customer website matters. All three include it.
  • Does a missed job alert cost you money? If yes, the technician needs the app. Choose option 2 or 3.
  • Do you need apps in the stores for trust or marketing? If yes, choose option 3.

These are estimates for discussion, not an offer. Provider prices and rates change. VAT and tax are extra. The agreed price and scope are fixed in the signed agreement.
