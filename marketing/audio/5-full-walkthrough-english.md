## Full text by section (paste each block into ElevenLabs as one clip)

### Title

This is NEW. One live system to sell air conditioners, bags and local cosmetics, and to dispatch field technicians across Dhaka. Five connected apps, one workflow.

### 1  Customer app

Start with the customer app: air conditioners, bags and local cosmetics, with a first-purchase offer always in view.
Services have fixed prices: repair, cooling problem, servicing, gas refill, installation and relocation.
In the shop, customers search, open the category menu, and sort from the filter sheet, so the screen stays clean.
Add to cart, Buy now, or save with a heart.
Each customer's history, searches, products viewed and orders, is kept on their own phone and nowhere else.

### 2  Booking

Now a real booking. The customer picks a service, a time slot and an address, and pays after the job.
Before the request goes through, the phone number is verified with a one-time code. Fake orders are stopped before they reach the team.
In the admin panel a count appears on Bookings straight away. Unread items are marked, and they turn read once someone opens them.

### 3  Dispatch

Admin opens the booking and sees the nearest technicians ranked by distance. If nobody is close, it goes to a vendor.
One tap assigns it. The customer is told at once, and every action is logged.
The live map shows technicians moving and vendors fixed.

### 4  Technician

A job offer arrives with a countdown. No answer in sixty seconds, and admin can reassign it.
The technician accepts, and the customer and admin see it at the same moment.
On the way, the technician follows live turn-by-turn directions. The customer and admin watch the same trip on the map. This uses open maps, so there is no paid map service.

### 5  The job

If extra work is needed on site, the technician picks a priced item from the list. There is no office approval to wait for, so the job keeps moving.
The customer decides in the app, and admin is informed.
The job can only be completed with the customer's four digit code. Our backend generates it and the customer app shows it. It is not an SMS.
Job done. And if the technician's app ever fails, admin can complete, cancel or override any step, and the log records exactly where it was done.

### 6  Reviews and support

After the job the customer rates the work, behaviour and punctuality. These ratings and comments are private to admin. Technicians and vendors never see them.
Customers can message admin inside the app, and admin replies from the Messages page. Once a booking is confirmed, the customer reaches the technician by phone call or WhatsApp.

### 7  Technician wallet

The technician wallet keeps two numbers: cash to deposit, and incentive earned. Cash collected stays on the balance until a deposit is accepted.
The technician submits a deposit. Admin, or the vendor for their own team, accepts it, and only then does the cash go to zero.
With cash cleared, the technician withdraws incentive. Finance pays it, all in an audit ledger.

### 8  Vendors

Where there is no NEW technician nearby, admin forwards the job to a vendor. Vendors have their own app and see only their own jobs, team and earnings.
The vendor assigns one of their own technicians. If the vendor does not reply in fifteen minutes, admin can forward the job to another vendor.
The admin mobile app does this on the go.

### 9  Shop and delivery

Back in the customer app, the same shop sells products with courier delivery.
Customers shop ACs, bags and cosmetics. Installation is an add-on with a discount and warranty.
Checkout is simple. Promo codes apply with one tap, items can be changed or removed right there, and the customer pays with cash on delivery first, then bKash, Nagad or card.
In Orders, admin packs the order and ships it with a courier partner. Each delivery update is one tap and shows up in the customer's timeline.
No paid courier connection is needed to start. Admin updates the status now, and updates can arrive from the courier automatically later.

### 10  Admin modules

Now the admin web panel. The dashboard shows bookings, jobs, revenue and what needs attention, live.
Admin adds services and changes prices and time slots, without a developer.
Products are bought from vendors and sold by NEW. Stock, price and discount are managed in one place.
Categories organise the shop. Promotions create offers and codes; provider offers can be added later.
The customers page lists every customer with their bookings and orders.
Technicians and vendors have their own pages. Vendor name, address and phone live here.
Payments shows every payment by method: cash on delivery, bKash, Nagad and card.
Earnings shows revenue, incentives and vendor commission at a glance.

### 11  Staff roles

Now staff roles. Admin can have many staff, and each person gets a role with its own permissions. Five ready roles are included.
Admin can also create new roles.
Operations executive: dispatch, overrides, extra-work pricing, orders and people. No finance.
Finance sees payments, collections, earnings and reviews. It cannot dispatch.
Catalog manager: services, products, promotions and orders only.
The Support agent handles messages and dispatch, so customers get quick answers.
The Super admin can do everything, including roles. Menus appear only when a role allows them.

### 12  Verified technicians

Every technician is verified before they can work. We capture national ID photos, run a live ID check and take a live selfie. Whoever onboards the technician, admin or a vendor, is responsible.
A vendor sees only its own technicians. Admin sees all.

### Close

That is NEW. Faster dispatch, fewer surprises, and full accountability, in one live system.
