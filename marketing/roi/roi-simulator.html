What makes it pay back sooner

  • Start selling on the Facebook page now. Blossom Classy already has about 5,000 followers and no products. Posting bags and clothing before launch builds the first buyers, so month 1 does not start from zero.
  • Launch before the hot season. AC service demand in Dhaka peaks from March to September. A launch in February or March catches it.
  • Bring known customers online. Offline company clients can stay offline, but their staff can book home AC service in the app.
  • Keep ads steady, not big. A small monthly budget that runs every month beats one large campaign.
  • Vendor commission is extra. Sales by other sellers on the marketplace earn NEW 8 percent. It is not counted here.
This is a model, not a promise. It uses assumptions about orders, growth and profit per sale that only real sales can confirm. Replace them with your own numbers. AC service follows the season (busier from March to September). The 36 months start at launch. VAT and tax are not included.
